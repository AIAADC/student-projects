## Interpretation notes

- Official status pages establish a standard's identity, status, and dates. Use the formal standard text for clause-level implementation.
- `GB/T 7713.1—2025` and `GB/T 7714—2025` adopt international standards; the official platform may provide only bibliographic information because of copyright. Obtain an authorized formal copy when exact clause interpretation is required.
- Industry standards `CY/T 170` and `CY/T 171` apply to academic books and journals; other publications may refer to them. Do not describe them as universal mandatory national standards.
- Copyright attribution and bibliographic citation are not substitutes for a license or permission.
